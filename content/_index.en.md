---
title: "Stories from a Northwest Village"
---

True stories from a village in northwest China: the village I grew up in, the village it has become, and the person who walked out of it.

Here I write about the land, the seasons, and the people — and about change: machines replacing oxen, young people moving to cities, old courtyards falling quiet.

If you grew up in a village too, you are welcome to share your story after reading.
